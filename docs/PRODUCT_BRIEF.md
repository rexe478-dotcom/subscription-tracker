# Product Brief

## Working description
A calm, trustworthy, global subscription-management PWA that helps people understand what they pay for, see what renews next, remember free-trial endings and manage cancellation actions without connecting their bank accounts.

## Problem
People use many recurring digital services and can lose track of renewal dates, trial conversions, billing amounts and cancellation destinations. A fragmented list or calendar reminder does not provide a useful overview.

## Product promise
Know what is active, what is coming up, what each service costs and what action is available — in one clear place.

## Target audience
Global consumers who manage multiple recurring digital subscriptions. Design for a broad audience, including people who use different currencies and a mixture of mobile and desktop devices.

## Product principles
1. Clarity over complexity.
2. Trust and user control over automation.
3. Accurate dates and currencies over flashy analytics.
4. Useful free core functionality.
5. Private by default; no bank connection required.
6. Every action and notification must be understandable.
7. Friendly brand personality without sacrificing professional usability.

## Release-one platform
Installable responsive PWA for Android, iOS and desktop. It is not a native App Store/Play Store app in v1.

## Working name
Undecided. Use a neutral internal project identifier such as `subscription-tracker`. Do not create final public branding until a name is selected.
