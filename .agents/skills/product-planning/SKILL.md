# Product Planning Skill

1. Read `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/SCOPE_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md` and `docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md`.
2. Restate the user problem and relevant acceptance criteria.
3. Separate v1 scope, future scope and non-goals.
4. Identify dependencies, risks and decisions that genuinely block progress.
5. Propose small milestones and measurable completion checks.
6. Update source documents when an approved product decision changes.
7. Do not add Gmail, payments, bank linking, household sharing or automatic cancellation to v1.
